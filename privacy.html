Snapshine privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Snapshine is a browser extension that turns screenshots into polished images. This policy explains what it does with your information.

Screenshots stay on your computer

When you capture part of a page, the image is processed inside your browser. Snapshine never uploads your screenshots, the pages you visit, or your browsing history anywhere. The capture is deleted from the extension’s storage as soon as the editor opens it.

What is stored locally

Snapshine saves your style choices (background, padding, colors) and a count of free exports in your browser’s local extension storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

Payments and license keys

Snapshine is sold through Polar (polar.sh), which acts as the merchant of record and processes payments. If you buy Snapshine, you give your email address and payment details to Polar, not to us. When you activate your license key, Snapshine sends the key to Polar’s servers to confirm it is valid, and checks it again about once a week. No screenshots or browsing data are sent. See Polar’s privacy policy for how it handles your data.

No selling or sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising or credit decisions.
